**Contagious Interview** (also tracked as WaterPlum, CL-STA-0240, Famous Chollima overlap, PurpleBravo, DeceptiveDevelopment, UNC5342, Tenacious Pungsan, and others) is a long-running North Korea–linked campaign that uses fake recruiters and fake job interviews to trick developers into running malware (BeaverTail, InvisibleFerret, OtterCookie, and later variants). First publicly named by Palo Alto Unit 42 in November 2023; activity dates to at least late 2022.

Below are the main reports and advisories with titles and links. This is not every news recap; it focuses on original research, vendor write-ups, and official advisories.

The September 2026 Japan/US/Australia/Germany advisory is the most recent official scale estimate: at least **30,000 devices** in 100+ countries (roughly Dec 2025–Jul 2026), credentials or funds from **7,000+ crypto wallets**, and about **JPY 1.7 billion (~USD 10.71 million)** moved to the DPRK. The group is assessed as operating under North Korea’s 313 General Bureau.

Wagemole / Famous Chollima IT-worker infiltration is often discussed alongside Contagious Interview but is a related, distinct line of activity (fake employees vs. fake interviews that drop malware).
